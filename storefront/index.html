Questions

Before you buy

Does it work on crew members' own phones?

Yes. WZOS runs in the browser and installs to the home screen on Android and iPhone, so crews can use the phone they already carry. Tablets and desktops work too.

Can I start with Core and upgrade later?

Yes. Core is designed as the first deployment. You can add dispatch, navigation, company forms and integrations later without moving your data.

Which safety forms are included?

The Forms hub ships with C85, JSA, DVIR, Incident and Whistleblower forms. Enterprise adds your company-specific forms and approval paths.

Is Atlas included in every edition?

Yes. The Atlas assistant is included in all builds and helps users log work orders, complete forms, check schedules and set up workflows.

Which apps does WZOS connect to?

Integrations include Slack, Microsoft Teams, Procore, QuickBooks, BambooHR and Zapier, among others. Integration planning is part of Enterprise.

How is payment handled?

Payment is handled by Stripe. Confirm the additional-user quantity and total before subscribing. Core and Enterprise activation is issued after verified payment and delivered inside your WZOS account. Stand-alone Time Tracker activation remains separate.

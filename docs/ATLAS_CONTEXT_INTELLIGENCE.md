# Atlas operational intelligence increment

Atlas receives bounded, read-only facts selected for the question: up to three
upcoming **own** assignments and three **own** saved forms, with totals and
truncation labels. Admin review questions may include organization review counts,
never other users' private form contents. Every query scopes the selected
organization and user; Core gets no Enterprise dispatch data.

No message bodies, employee HR/contact details, assessment answer keys, file URLs,
credentials or free-text incident notes enter this new context. Existing selected
job/source context remains subject to existing permissions and revision checks.
Missing forms are not findings of noncompliance. Unsubmitted means only that the
current saved revision has no internal submission; it says nothing about agency
filing or work completed outside WZOS. Recommendations do not perform actions.

Capability guidance is brought up to date with assembled messaging, meetings,
training, dispatch and navigation. Provider-dependent delivery and qualification
claims remain distinct. The model stays unchanged; this is context/reasoning
integration, not weight training or proof of increased live model accuracy.
No DDL, paid provisioning, customer sends or deployment.
